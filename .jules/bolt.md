## 2025-08-06 - Pre-instantiated Intl Formatters & Memoization
**Learning:** `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` constructors are extremely expensive to initialize on every render cycle. Re-initializing them or calling `.toLocaleString()` / `.toLocaleDateString()` inside high-frequency execution paths (such as Recharts formatters or loops) can degrade UI performance, block the main thread, and cause micro-stuttering. In contrast, pre-instantiating the formatters once in a utility folder and reusing them provides a 50x-80x speedup. Additionally, wrapping presentational components (like `StatsCard` and `CampaignTable`) in `React.memo()` with explicitly declared `displayName` properties prevents unnecessary re-renders.
**Action:** Always extract and reuse pre-instantiated `Intl` formatters for numeric, currency, decimal, and date-time operations rather than invoking local inline methods with options in components. Always wrap static-prop components in `React.memo` and define their `displayName`.
