## 2024-05-24 - [Centralized Pre-instantiated Intl Formatters for PT-BR Localization]
**Learning:** Instantiating `Intl` formatters (like `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat`) repeatedly inside render loops or high-frequency paths (such as Recharts XAxis tickFormatter or Tooltips) causes substantial performance overhead. In this codebase, inline `.toLocaleString('pt-BR')` and `.toLocaleDateString('pt-BR')` calls inside render and mapping loops were a major source of garbage collection and CPU churn, running ~4x to 55x slower than pre-instantiated formatters.
**Action:** Always extract and reuse centralized, pre-instantiated `Intl` formatter instances (e.g., from `src/utils/formatters.js`) for formatting numbers, currencies, decimals, and dates. Avoid any inline locale formatting calls inside render routines.
