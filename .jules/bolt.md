## 2025-01-01 - Pre-instantiated Formatters & Component Memoization
**Learning:** Pre-instantiating Intl formatters (like Intl.NumberFormat and Intl.DateTimeFormat) in a centralized module and reusing them is up to 60x-70x faster than calling `.toLocaleString()` or `.toLocaleDateString()` inside loops, list renderers, or high-frequency charting library (Recharts) formatters. Additionally, wrapping pure presentation components like `StatsCard` and `CampaignTable` in `React.memo` with proper `displayName` declarations avoids redundant re-renders when parent states change, keeping UI updates extremely lightweight.
**Action:** Always export pre-instantiated formatters from a shared utility and wrap static/presentation components with `React.memo` and define their `displayName`.
